A website for your school

Every public school, college and institute of the ISEZ may have an address on the network free of charge.

Standard page

The school fills in Form EN-1 (name, head, address, telephone, number of pupils, languages of teaching, a photograph of the building) and hands it to the district or city education office. The network centre opens the page within one month at name.iradedu.org. Changes: Form EN-2. Since 2019 a school may add sections (history, news, teachers, timetable, enrolment, contacts) with Form EN-3. Example: Teskey-Jar Basic School.

Own website

A college, lyceum or school may make its own site and place it on the network. Conditions:

  1. the site is made and kept up by the institution;
  2. it names the institution and the Ministry of Education and links to iradedu.org;
  3. no advertising except the institution's own sponsors and partners;
  4. photographs of pupils only with the parents' consent;
  5. the head of the institution answers for its contents.
