News

Autumn holidays · 30.10.2026
Autumn holidays in all public schools are 1–8 November. The 2nd quarter begins on Monday 9 November. During the holidays the network centre answers on 23-15-31 from 9:00 to 13:00.
New thematic plans for History of Irad, grades 5–7 · 21.10.2026
The Research Institute of Pedagogy has posted the 2026–2027 thematic plans for History of Irad in grades 5, 6 and 7. Download from the Institute's site.
Navbahor school connected · 06.10.2026
The new Navbahor school in Nurzhan, opened on 5 October by Lieutenant Governor Maksat, has been connected to the network: 24 classrooms, a computer room of 16 places and a fibre line from Attelkom. Its page will be opened when the school sends Form EN-1.
Directive 2026/31 on textbooks · 26.09.2026
The Directive sets aside at least IS 41 million for printing school textbooks in 2027–2030. Every new title will pass an expert review. What it means for schools.
The school year has begun · 01.09.2026
About 400,000 pupils went back to school on 1 September. Schools that changed their head or telephone over the summer are asked to update their page (Form EN-2).
Graduation Test 2026: results published · 14.07.2026
Results of the National Graduation Test 2026 by candidate number and by school are on e-Irad. Appeals against a score go to the Education Quality Agency within 10 days.
School mail moves to iradedu.org · 17.03.2025
School mailboxes have moved to the network's new mail server. Addresses @edu.id.gov are forwarded until 31 December 2025 and then closed. Passwords are given out by the district education offices.

Earlier news

From the Ministry's pages before iradedu.org and the first years of the network.

Pages in Iradi first · 04.09.2017
Standard pages of schools that teach every subject in Iradi now open in Iradi; the English version is one click away. The School Terminological Dictionary of the Research Institute of Pedagogy is used for the page headings.
Teachers' forum closed · 12.10.2015
The teachers' forum is closed because of spam. Questions about methods go to the Institute's methodists by e-mail.
The network moves to iradedu.org · 01.09.2011
Every school, college and institute can now have its own address, name.iradedu.org. Schools without a site of their own get a standard page, filled in by the school on Form EN-1.
School uniform from 1 September · 19.08.2011
Under the Ministry's directive, pupils of public schools wear the navy uniform with a white shirt from the new school year. Schools may keep their own badge.
Graduation Test 2009: languages of the papers · 10.02.2009
From this year the National Graduation Test is set in Iradi or in English. Candidates choose the language when they register at their school.
First Graduation Test: results by school · 23.06.2005
Results of the first National Graduation Test are on the Ministry's page, by candidate number and by school.
School Connection Project: the first 40 schools online · 15.09.2003
With Attelkom, the Ministry has connected 40 city schools by dial-up. Each school has a mailbox at edu.id.gov and a page on the Ministry's site. Passwords are given out by the district education offices.
