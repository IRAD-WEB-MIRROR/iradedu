For teachers

Materials for teachers of the public schools. Curricula, thematic plans and textbooks are prepared by the Research Institute of Pedagogy and approved by the Ministry.

Courses are free for teachers of public schools; travel and lodging for district teachers are paid by the district education office. Apply through your head of school.

Work and pay

  • Vacant teaching posts (about 1,400 posts were vacant in 2025, most for English and physics in the districts). Teachers who go to a district school receive the housing allowance.
  • Questions on pay and the teaching load: the trade union of education workers, through your school's union representative.
