Graduation Test

Results 2026 by candidate number and by school are published by the Education Quality Agency on the e-Irad portal: e-irad.org/test-results/. The network does not keep copies of the results.

The National Graduation Test (Uluttuk Sınak) ends grade 12 and has been set since 2005 by the Education Quality Agency of the Office of the Governor. Candidates sit four compulsory subjects (Iradi language and literature, mathematics, history of Irad, English) and one elective, in Iradi or in English, in June at the test points in the cities and district centres. Each subject is marked out of 100 and the total out of 500; the pass mark is 36 in each subject.

Points are used for admission to Talas State University, the Nurzhan Polytechnic Institute and the colleges, and for the state-funded grant places (about 30% of university places).

For schools

  • Results by school are shown on e-Irad by city or district. Heads of schools receive the full table for their school from the district education office.
  • Appeals against a score: in writing to the Education Quality Agency within 10 days of publication.
  • External candidates (no ISEZ school certificate) sit the Test at the test points of Talas, Iyık Janyl, Tash-Kömür and Bazar-Korgon.
  • Talas American School does not take part (US curriculum).

Test 2027

The 2027 Test will be held in June 2027. Dates, test points and the list of electives will be announced by the Agency in March 2027 and copied here.

Earlier results (2014–2025) are given by the Agency on request.
