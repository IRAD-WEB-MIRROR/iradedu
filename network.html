About the network

The Education Network of the ISEZ is run by the Education Network Centre of the Ministry of Education. It connects schools, colleges and institutes to the Internet, keeps their mail and gives each of them a page or a website on iradedu.org.

History

15 September 2003. The School Connection Project of the Ministry and Attelkom links the first 40 city schools by dial-up. Schools get mailboxes at edu.id.gov and a page on the ministry's site.

2007. All secondary schools of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl and the district centres are connected; the Attelkom ADSL line reaches the colleges.

1 September 2011. The network moves to its own address, iradedu.org, on the standard template of the Department of Public Information. Every institution can have its own address: name.iradedu.org. Institutions that do not make their own site get the standard page, filled in by the school.

12 October 2015. The teachers' forum is closed. 4 September 2017. Pages of the schools that teach every subject in Iradi are opened in Iradi first.

2024. New design of the portal, made by the centre; the old template stays only on the standard pages.

17 March 2025. New mail server; the edu.id.gov addresses are closed at the end of the year.
